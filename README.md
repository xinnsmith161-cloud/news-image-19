A newspaper front page summing up Thursday, 19 November 2026, built for the
[Sum up 19 November's world news in one image bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119)
on make.fun. The program (`main.ts`, Node 22 standard library only) copies
`entry.png` — a broadsheet front page drawn with PIL — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

## Stories covered

- **Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — first-ever three-way summit ends
  with a four-point "Declaration toward Peace", but no mention of the
  Ukraine or Iran wars.
- **Court: Myanmar committed genocide** — the UN's top court (ICJ) rules
  Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, 9–6, in the case
  brought by Gambia.
- **Anthropic prices record IPO** — shares priced at $83, valuing the AI
  company at about $1.5 trillion; Nasdaq trading expected Thursday.
- **Bangladesh in turmoil** — violence in 28 districts; Muhammad Yunus's
  ancestral home set on fire amid protests by Sheikh Hasina's party.
- **Also today** — Hormuz tanker attacks mount as US–Iran strikes escalate;
  Van Gogh sells for $190.4 million, a record for the artist; US and
  Venezuela sign a military cooperation deal; US Senate rejects the Iran
  war-powers resolution, 51–48.
